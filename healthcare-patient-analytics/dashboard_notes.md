# Power BI and Tableau Dashboard Plan

## Page 1 - Patient Overview

KPI cards:
- Total Patient Visits
- Average Waiting Time
- 30-Day Readmission Rate
- Average Satisfaction Score

Recommended visuals:
- Monthly patient visits trend
- Department-wise patient volume
- Visit type distribution
- Patient age-group distribution

## Page 2 - Department Performance

Recommended visuals:
- Average waiting time by department
- Readmission rate by department
- Average length of stay by department
- Satisfaction score by department

## Filters

- Department
- Visit Type
- Gender
- Visit Month

## Power BI

Use the cleaned CSV files generated by `analysis.py`. Create measures for total visits, average waiting time and readmission rate, then add slicers for department and visit type.

## Tableau

Connect Tableau to the same cleaned patient dataset. Build worksheets for monthly visits, department performance and readmission rate, then combine them into an interactive dashboard.
