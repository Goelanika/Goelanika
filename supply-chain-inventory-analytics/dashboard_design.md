# Power BI Dashboard Design

## Inventory Overview
Cards: Inventory Value, Average Turnover, Days of Inventory, Stock-out Rate and A-Class SKUs.
Charts: category sales value, stock-out rate by ABC class and movement segment.

## SKU Analysis
ABC x movement matrix, high-value stock-out watchlist and slow-moving excess inventory table.

## Supplier Scorecard
On-time delivery, defect rate, average lead time and supplier drill-through.

Slicers: category, supplier, ABC class and movement segment.
