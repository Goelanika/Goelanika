# Power BI Dashboard Design

## Executive Overview
Cards: Customers, Churn Rate, Active Member Rate and High-Risk Customers.
Charts: churn by risk segment, tenure, activity status and product count.

## Segment Explorer
Matrix: geography x tenure band. Drill-through customer table with non-sensitive synthetic identifiers.

## Retention Actions
Prioritize inactive high-balance customers, new customers with early churn signals and customers holding three or more products.
